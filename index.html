What is “Korean age”?

Three ways Korea has counted age

International age is the age most of the world uses: 0 at birth, +1 on each birthday. Korean counting age starts at 1 at birth and everyone adds a year on January 1st, so a baby born on December 31 turns 2 the next day. Year age is simply this year minus your birth year.

What changed in June 2023?

From June 28, 2023, South Korea standardized on international age for legal and administrative use. A few rules (for example some youth-protection and military-service rules) still count by year, and many people still use counting age in daily conversation.

Why is my Korean age 1–2 years higher?

Counting age adds 1 at birth and adds the next year on January 1st instead of on your birthday — so before your birthday it is 2 higher than international age, after your birthday 1 higher.
